# Notable NPCs of Eberron

## Campaign 1 Heroes

### Astra, The Dreamer
Astra is a goddess who now oversees the Dream Realm (Dal Quor) and the Quori. She ascended to divinity on New Year's Eve 999 YK after the Dream Team defeated the original Dreamer—the collective Quori godmind. Originally from Toril (the world of Faerûn), Astra was a homunculus who could transform into her true dragon form. Her connection to dreams, The Fury, and the Quori deepened throughout Campaign 1, culminating in her absorbing divine power during the final battle in the Mournland.

As a foreign entity who ascended to godhood in Eberron's cosmology, she represents an unprecedented situation. The Chamber views her as an illegitimate interloper and fears cosmic instability from her rule. She has been goddess for approximately 30 years—a mere eyeblink compared to other divine entities. She rarely intervenes directly in mortal affairs, though her influence stabilized Dal Quor and caused the Mournland to slowly recede over the following decades.

The Draconic Prophecy contains troubling references that The Chamber interprets as warnings about her reign. Whether their fears are justified or self-fulfilling remains to be seen.

---

### Snitt Trist
Snitt is a red kobold artificer and one of the legendary Dream Team who saved Eberron. Father to Praetor Lyralei Siberclaw, he married Xevoa (a gold kobold bard) after the events of Campaign 1. Together they ran Bookworm's Treasure, the magic item shop in Sharn where the Dream Team once researched their adventures.

Known for his obsession with House Cannith propaganda comics and his mechanical genius, Snitt discovered hidden prophecies within the comics and eventually helped defeat a clockwork dragon above Sharn. His relationship with Shavala the gold kobold blossomed during Campaign 1, and together they raised Lyralei.

Around 1020 YK, Snitt and Xevoa disappeared into the Feywild to explore—a journey connected to Lyralei's own research into Feywild manifest zones. Their current whereabouts are unknown, though Lyralei maintains hope they'll return. His legacy lives on through his daughter's work at Alae Draconis.

---

### Loona (Luna)
Luna is a former member of the Dream Team who became a renowned treasure hunter and explorer after the events of Campaign 1. Her wealth, accumulated through decades of successful expeditions, provided significant funding for the construction of Alae Draconis between 1005-1025 YK.

During Campaign 1, Luna claimed the power of the Phoenix from the Plane of Fire, gaining control over her element plus enhanced abilities. She participated in the final battle against The Dreamer, where her disintegration spell destroyed the collective Quori godmind and enabled Astra's ascension.

Her contributions to Alae Draconis were primarily financial, though her connections across Khorvaire also helped establish the diplomatic frameworks necessary for the academy's founding. She maintains a reputation as one of the greatest adventurers of her generation.

---

### Mutt
Mutt was a member of the Dream Team with a deep connection to The Devourer, one of the Dark Six. During Campaign 1, Mutt claimed the power of the Zaratan from the Plane of Earth. In the final battle, The Devourer attempted to possess Mutt to claim all the gathered elemental power for himself.

The party was forced to kill Mutt to stop The Devourer's plan—then resurrected him afterward. Mutt returned bearing the Mark of Death, a dragonmark thought extinct since the destruction of House Vol. The implications of this mark's return remain significant to Eberron's political and magical landscape.

His current activities and whereabouts are less documented than his fellow Dream Team members, though the Mark of Death ensures he remains a figure of considerable interest to various factions.

---

### Xevoa
Xevoa is a gold kobold bard and the mother of Praetor Lyralei Siberclaw. She married Snitt Trist after Campaign 1, and together they operated Bookworm's Treasure in Sharn while raising their daughter.

Her love of knowledge and storytelling influenced Lyralei's scholarly nature, while Snitt's artificer brilliance shaped their daughter's technical abilities. Around 1020 YK, Xevoa and Snitt departed for the Feywild to explore, leaving Lyralei to continue their legacy through her work at Alae Draconis.

The notes from their Feywild explorations have given Lyralei unique insights into the island's ruins and manifest zones—knowledge she applies to her revolutionary planar engineering work.

## Daughters of Sora Kell

### Sora Katra
Sora Katra is a green hag and one of the three Daughters of Sora Kell who rule the nation of Droaam. She serves as the group's voice and strategist—a legendary schemer who maintains a network of agents called Daask throughout cities in Aundair and Breland.

A skilled orator and bard, Sora Katra uses various magical means including hag eyes and circles of sound to stay in contact with her minions. She works with House Tharashk to spread Droaam's monstrous mercenaries across Khorvaire, legitimizing Droaam's place in international politics.

She has worn a thousand faces and more, each story calling for something new. Her diplomatic skill was instrumental in the treaties that ended hostilities between the monster races and human nations following Campaign 1. She helped negotiate Droaam's involvement in founding Alae Draconis.

---

### Sora Maenya
Sora Maenya is an annis hag and one of the Daughters of Sora Kell who rule Droaam. Before her alliance with her sisters, she terrorized the Eldeen Reaches. The opportunity for bigger, better, and more meaningful fights was the main reason she agreed to Sora Teraza's proposal to form Droaam.

A powerful barbarian of legendary strength, Maenya is not incapable of using the natural guile and slyness of hagkind to her advantage. In one infamous story, she posed as a Brelish refugee at a military outpost, then systematically devoured the soldiers one by one. She binds the souls of those she kills into their skulls.

Despite her brutal nature, she has proven an effective ruler alongside her sisters, providing the military might that backs Droaam's diplomatic negotiations.

---

### Sora Teraza
Sora Teraza is a dusk hag and one of the Daughters of Sora Kell—the blind wanderer of the Demon Wastes who conceived the plan to create Droaam. Before convincing her sisters to unite, she wandered the Demon Wastes seeking ancient knowledge.

She possesses oracular powers that allow her to perceive her surroundings despite her blindness. It is said she has shelves of books containing the actual lives of important individuals—not records, but the lives themselves. She has powers like those of a cleric but has never shown devotion to any god.

Years ago, Sora Teraza studied dragonshard fields with House Tharashk support. After a troll attack during an escort through what became Droaam, the Tharashk siblings recognized the potential for monstrous mercenaries—leading to Droaam's current economic foundation.

---

### Sora Kell
Sora Kell is a great night hag of legend and the mother of the three Daughters who rule Droaam. She has traveled continents and planes seeking ultimate mystical power. Hundreds of legends exist about her, some contradicting others—in some she is a fierce wizard, in others she rends whole armies with her claws.

Some rumors claim she is an agent of The Traveler; others propose she actually IS The Traveler. At some point, she gave birth to three daughters: Katra (green hag), Maenya (annis hag), and Teraza (dusk hag). Their half-fiendish traits suggest their father was a demon, devil, or rakshasa.

There have been no confirmed sightings of Sora Kell within the last century. Some say she watches her daughters from afar; others say she works on a great spell; others say she is trapped on another plane. Her current fate remains one of Eberron's great mysteries.

## Cairn Port NPCs

### Valera "Val" Threndar
Valera Threndar runs The Salty Wench tavern in Cairn Port with an iron fist and sharp eye for trouble. A descendant of House Vol refugees who fled to Farlnen generations ago, she maintains old traditions while adapting to practical needs. She lost her leg in a pirate raid decades ago and now walks with an elven-crafted prosthetic of polished darkwood and mithral—which she has used as a weapon when necessary.

Her silver-white hair is kept short and practical, and her piercing blue eyes miss nothing. The magical prosthetic makes no sound as she moves—a trait that has surprised many troublemakers. A small tattoo on her left wrist shows the Blood of Vol symbol, usually kept hidden.

As a follower of the Blood of Vol, she believes in self-reliance and personal responsibility. She pays Bloodsail taxes on time, keeps out of clan politics, and expects patrons to follow her rules: no fighting inside, no cheating at tables (unless very good at it), and no disrespecting Bloodsails where they might hear.

---

### Silvian "Silks" Vadallia
Silvian "Silks" Vadallia is a half-elf who runs gambling operations in The Salty Wench's back rooms. Charming, smooth-talking, and sharp as a knife, he earned his nickname both for his fine clothes and his smooth handling of difficult situations.

Handsome in a rakish way, with slicked-back dark hair and warm brown eyes that sparkle with humor, he dresses far better than most in Cairn Port. His fingers are quick and graceful—equally skilled at dealing cards and picking pockets (though he claims he gave up the latter years ago).

He manages the gambling rooms, ensures games run smoothly, collects house percentage, and delivers the Bloodsails' cut. He particularly enjoys when wealthy marks or overconfident sailors think they can beat the house. He remembers every face, every game, and every debt.

---

### Hot Guy
Hot Guy (as he's known to adventurers who met him years ago) is an elven fisherman who operates a small boat out of Lake Home. He spends his days on the water, fishing and occasionally ferrying travelers.

A chill, laid-back individual content with his simple life, he's friendly to travelers and doesn't ask too many questions. His easygoing nature and exceptionally good looks have made him somewhat legendary among those who pass through.

During Campaign 1, he had a memorable encounter with the Dream Team when he was accidentally dragged to the lake's depths. They created a magical bubble allowing him to reach the surface. Despite the near-death experience, he bears no ill will and still operates his boat service. Years later, he's still out on the water, still hot, still chill.

---

### Ren Velun, The Stuff Magistrate
Ren Velun is a 37-year-old human merchant who holds the self-important title of "Stuff Magistrate." He deals primarily in furs, minerals, and ores, traveling between ports with his cargo.

Overconfident, somewhat pompous, and not particularly bright despite his financial success, Ren is impressive at first glance (physically attractive and well-dressed) but quickly becomes less appealing once he starts talking. He's prone to showing off and making grandiose gestures.

He lost his three-person sloop (dubbed "the Stuff Sloop") to Rynel, Soryn, and Sylwen in a gambling match at The Salty Wench. The party won his boat in a single round of Liar's Dice. He was allowed to keep his valuable cargo by the generous victors, meaning he now owes them a significant favor. His merchant connections and travel experience could prove valuable when that favor is called in.

## Sharn Underworld

### Elas Daetoris, Queen of the Cogs
Elas Daetoris is a Drow who clawed her way from mid-level black market dealer to undisputed Queen of the Cogs in Sharn. Approximately 450 years old, she is the adopted mother of Rynel Daetoris, one of the current Alae Draconis students.

Thirty years ago, she maintained a lucrative working relationship with Snitt Trist of the Dream Team. When their heroics left trade networks fractured, Elas seized the chaos, expanding from fencing stolen relics and black market weapons into smuggling, extortion, and assassination. She united her syndicate under the name The Iron Wing.

From a ruined foundry repurposed as her throne room, she directs spies, killers, and smugglers with quiet precision. A master of patience and leverage, she prefers debts and favors over bloodshed when possible. Despite her imposing nature, she is surprisingly motherly with her underlings. When the opportunity arose, she helped the deans of Alae Draconis select promising candidates from among her people—including her adopted son.

Whispers claim she still keeps a ledger of old debts that could shatter her empire if revealed. She holds strange respect for the Dream Team, perhaps remembering her partnership with Snitt.

## Cosmic Entities

### The Traveler
The Traveler is one of the Dark Six—the gods cast out by the Sovereign Host. Associated with chaos, change, deception, and innovation, The Traveler is said to appear in many forms and is never depicted the same way twice.

During Campaign 1, The Traveler appeared to the Dream Team in the form of an obsidian dragon, rescuing them from nightmares after their battle with the clockwork dragon above Sharn. This intervention proved crucial to their eventual success.

Some legends claim Sora Kell is an agent of The Traveler; others propose she actually IS The Traveler in another form. The truth, as with most things involving this deity, remains deliberately obscured.
