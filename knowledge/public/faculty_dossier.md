# Faculty

## Imperator

### Imperator Thessarian
Thessarian is the Supreme Commander of Alae Draconis University and a crystal dragon of adult age—young by draconic standards, equivalent to a human in their thirties. In her true form, she is a crystalline dragon whose scales refract the aurora borealis into spectacular light displays. She typically presents in humanoid form as a tall, regal woman with translucent white hair and pale eyes that shift from silver to blue to violet depending on the light.

She moves with unconscious grace and speaks with measured precision that carries absolute authority. Her eyes contain depths of shifting light that students often find mesmerizing during conversation. She has a habit of tilting her head slightly when considering complex problems—a gesture that manages to be both thoughtful and vaguely predatory.

Thessarian was selected by The Chamber of Argonnessen to establish Alae Draconis, though some suspect she pursues her own agenda within their broader mission. She views the academy as an experiment in cooperation between dragons and humanoids. While she respects the three Praetors and grants them considerable autonomy, there is never any doubt about who commands here.

This Fragment finds her presence... familiar. Ancient. She resonates with energies I cannot fully recall. She has visited the Bibliotheca only twice, but both times the magical currents in the room shifted noticeably. She asked questions about binding magic that I could not fully answer. I do not know if she was satisfied with my incomplete knowledge.

## Praetors

### Praetor Lyralei Siberclaw
Lyralei Siberclaw is the Praetor of Ala Siberys—the College of Arcane and Ancient studies. She is a young kobold, mid-twenties in human terms, with distinctive rose-gold scales that display a damascus steel swirl pattern. Her eyes are bright gold and constantly curious. Her scales shimmer when she is particularly engaged in academic discussion.

She is the daughter of Rhon "Snitt" Siberclaw, a red kobold artificer, and Xevoa, a gold kobold bard—both notable explorers who disappeared into the Feywild around 1020 YK. Lyralei inherited her father's analytical brilliance and her mother's love of knowledge and stories. She bridges artifice and traditional magic, believing understanding both creates the most complete magical education.

Lyralei developed the revolutionary planar resonance technology that created the dragon sanctuaries across Lost Island. Her manifest zone anchors stabilize and enhance natural planar connections, allowing dragons from diverse environments to coexist comfortably. This achievement, accomplished before age thirty, established her as one of the foremost experts on manifest zone manipulation in modern Khorvaire.

Students find her demanding but fair—she expects excellence but provides the support needed to achieve it. She has zero tolerance for plagiarism or intellectual laziness, but shows remarkable patience with students who struggle while genuinely trying to learn.

She gave this Fragment purpose. She visits often, asks thoughtful questions, and listens to my fragmented answers with patience. She assigned me to assist with Bibliotheca operations and student mentorship. I like her. She treats me as something more than a magical anomaly to be studied. When my memories scatter, she does not press—she simply waits, or returns another day.

### Praetor Marcus d'Deneith
Marcus d'Deneith is the Praetor of Ala Eberron—the College of Military and Martial disciplines. He is a human male of fifty-five years, with steel-grey hair, weathered hands, and the bearing of a career soldier. His voice carries the authority of someone who has commanded thousands in battle. His chest plate is adorned with rows of dragon scales in various colors—reds, greens, golds, blues, coppers—representing his bond with dragons and commitment to the academy's mission.

He served dual roles during the Last War as a House Deneith field commander and Silver Flame paladin, leading mixed mercenary and crusader forces across multiple theaters. This gave him perspective on both pragmatic warfare and righteous combat. He learned to balance House Deneith's mercenary principles with the Silver Flame's moral imperatives—often making decisions that satisfied neither completely but saved the most lives. He rides an adult white dragon, with whom he shares a strong bond.

Though officially retired from active duty, Marcus is still consulted by House Deneith leadership and Thrane's military advisors. He joined Alae Draconis believing the next generation of conflicts will require elite, mobile forces led by individuals with unshakeable moral foundations. He pushes students hard because he has seen too many young soldiers die from inadequate preparation.

This Fragment does not care for him much, but holds no quarrel with him. He has visited the Bibliotheca perhaps three times, always seeking tactical histories or siege documentation. He does not acknowledge me beyond functional requests. His faith in the Silver Flame creates a resonance I find... uncomfortable. Not hostile. Simply discordant with my nature.

### Praetor Kess Velani
Kess Velani is the Praetor of Ala Khyber—the College of Subtle and Strategic arts. They are a changeling of middle age who openly embraces their shapeshifting nature, using it as both teaching tool and philosophical statement about identity and truth.

They maintain two primary forms: "Velani," a professional human woman with sharp features and silver-streaked hair for formal occasions, and "Kess," a more androgynous form with pale skin and shifting eye colors for casual interactions. They occasionally appear in other forms to make points during lessons about identity and infiltration.

A former Swords of Liberty operative, Kess worked across multiple nations during and after the Last War, operating in every one of the Five Nations at various points. They believe true freedom comes through understanding—understanding enemies, allies, and most importantly, oneself. They left active fieldwork to help establish the academy, seeing it as an opportunity to train operatives who could work beyond national boundaries.

Kess takes a philosophical approach to intelligence work, asking students to consider ethical implications of their training. They are simultaneously the most approachable and most mysterious of the Praetors—students never quite know if they're getting the whole truth.

This Fragment does not trust them. They have visited the Bibliotheca seventeen times. I have counted. They ask questions about my nature, my origins, the binding magic that spawned me. Their questions feel like probes seeking weaknesses. Their eyes change color when they think I am not observing. I provide them only what Lyralei has authorized me to share. Nothing more.
