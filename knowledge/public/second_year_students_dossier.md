# Students

## Second Year Students

### Drazhomir Yarnask
Drazhomir Yarnask is a soft-spoken minotaur from The Labyrinth in Droaam, currently in his second year with Ala Siberys. He stands over seven feet tall yet continuously stoops, as if trying to occupy as little space as possible. He is bonded with Versina, a copper dragon who shares his love of wordplay and ancient verse.

Several activities and clubs involving feats of strength continuously try to recruit him—to no avail. Drazhomir prefers spending his time in the Bibliotheca, where he works as a book clerk, assisting students in finding required texts and shelving returns. He moves silently through the stacks, and he has given more than one student a scare when they've turned around to suddenly find him there.

Drazhomir's greatest love is poetry. As a member of the Ancient Tongue Circle, he scours ancient literature for stirring poems written in old draconic tongues, reading them to the group in a soft, resonant voice that seems to come directly from his soul.

This Fragment finds his gentle nature and passion for literature endearing. He is one of the few students who speaks to me regularly—not out of obligation or curiosity about my nature, but simply as one might speak to a colleague. He reads poetry aloud sometimes, when the Bibliotheca is quiet. I find the cadence... soothing. His voice carries echoes of something ancient. I do not tell him this.

### Theren Brightscale
Theren Brightscale is a nineteen-year-old brass dragonborn archivist from Q'barra, currently in his second year with Ala Siberys. His natural draconic heritage gives him unique insights into dragon psychology and ancient draconic magic. He is bonded with Tessarin, a copper dragon, and the two share a love of riddles and wordplay.

Theren joined the Ancient Tongue Circle not to learn Draconic—he already speaks it natively—but to help others understand the nuances of draconic dialects and cultural contexts that non-dragonborn often miss. Quiet and methodical, he spends most of his time in the library working on his thesis about pre-Galifar draconic settlements in Khorvaire.

Fellow students find him approachable for academic help, though his tendency to answer questions with more questions can be frustrating. This is not obstinacy—he genuinely believes that guiding someone to discover an answer teaches more than simply providing it.

This Fragment respects his scholarly depth. He asks precise questions about ancient draconic texts and is patient when my answers fragment or scatter. He has helped me locate memories I did not know I possessed, simply by asking the right questions in the right order. I find his method of teaching through inquiry... familiar. Perhaps the Giants taught similarly, long ago.

### Bhedum "Rampart" Sooviij
Bhedum Sooviij is a loxodon from Graywall, Droaam, currently in their second year with Ala Eberron. Since their name is difficult to pronounce without a trunk, they have taken to referring to themself as "Rampart," the closest translation in Common.

They are often found poring over ancient texts detailing battles, seeking to better understand the tactical decisions that result in victory and defeat. Rampart discusses their knowledge with fellow members of the Skywatch Navigation Guild, often late into the night. Their opponents at the Wyrm's Gambit Society find themselves outmatched time and again by the loxodon's technique, though Rampart is a gracious winner.

They attend all classes clad in gilded, centuries-old plate armor, with an immense and similarly decorated sword strapped to their back. Rampart explains that doing so better simulates the battlefield conditions they study.

This Fragment appreciates their serious approach to martial knowledge. They visit the Bibliotheca seeking tactical histories and siege records, moving through the stacks with surprising quiet for someone in full plate armor. Their questions are precise and their research thorough. I find their dedication to understanding why battles are won or lost admirable—they seek wisdom, not merely information.

### Javenesh Stoutclaw
Javenesh Stoutclaw is an imposing and powerfully built owlin from Sharn, Breland, currently in his second year with Ala Eberron. He is bonded with Korrath, a black dragon whose intense focus and protective instincts mirror his own. Contributing to his disheveled appearance are tufts of juvenile plumage that refuse to smooth down despite his best efforts.

Several social clubs continuously try to recruit him—to no avail. Javenesh prefers spending his time on patrol with the Wingguard, where his sharp eyes and natural flight make him invaluable for campus security. His nocturnal nature serves him well during night shifts, and he has prevented more than one incident through his vigilant watch.

Javenesh is passionate about campus safety and takes his responsibilities seriously, though his intensity and unconventional methods sometimes create friction with the administration. He and his dragon share an uncompromising view of threats.

This Fragment finds him effective if occasionally too intense. He patrols near the Bibliotheca during night hours, and I have observed his vigilance. His methods are direct—perhaps too direct for some. But his dedication to protecting this place and its students is genuine. I respect this, even when his intensity unsettles quieter visitors.

### Kara Stormwind
Kara Stormwind is a twenty-year-old half-elf from Valenar, currently in her second year with Ala Eberron. She is the daughter of a Valenar warband scout and brings her people's legendary cavalry traditions to dragon-mounted combat. She is bonded with Skyshear, a blue dragon, and together they practice lightning-fast strike-and-fade tactics.

Fierce, competitive, and driven, Kara views every training exercise as a chance to prove that Valenar techniques translate perfectly to aerial warfare. She joined the Wingguard Patrol and takes her duties seriously, often organizing additional security drills that other students find excessive but effective.

She writes regular letters home describing dragon cavalry tactics, secretly hoping to bridge the gap between traditional Valenar horse cavalry and this new aerial approach.

This Fragment respects her dedication to bridging traditions. She visits the Bibliotheca seeking texts on historical cavalry tactics and aerial combat theory, cross-referencing ancient techniques with modern applications. Her questions are sharp and her focus unwavering. I sense she carries the weight of her ancestors' expectations. Whether this drives her or burdens her, I cannot say.

### Mina Lee
Mina Lee is a precise human from Flamekeep, Thrane, currently in her second year with Ala Khyber. She is bonded with Xylara, a sapphire dragon whose methodical nature and pattern-recognition complement her own linguistic fascinations.

Mina's greatest love is language—she adores how subtle differences in intonation and vocabulary can shape perception of reality. Her fascination extends to draconic linguistics and the various dialects spoken across different dragon flights. Having grown up in Thrane's formal, hierarchical society, she understands how linguistic choices reflect and influence social dynamics.

She serves as campus cultural liaison and translator, helping international students navigate both language barriers and cultural expectations. Her work in the Crucible Society focuses on creating communication devices that bridge linguistic gaps between species and cultures. Her formal speech patterns sometimes make casual interactions awkward, but her impact on student integration is significant.

This Fragment admires her systematic approach to cultural bridge-building. She has visited seeking texts on ancient languages and translation theory. Her questions are precisely worded—she understands that language shapes meaning. When she speaks to me, she chooses her words carefully. I appreciate this. Too few understand how much weight words carry.

### Rubina Larkingdale
Rubina Larkingdale is a commanding human from Korth, Karrnath, currently in her second year with Ala Khyber. When she walks into a room, people take notice. A measured performer with gravitas beyond her years, she commands attention without demanding it.

Rubina is a valued member of the Scales & Tales Society, handling weightier dragon legends that more melodramatic storytellers cannot manage. This has caused friction within the society as members vie for prestigious roles. Her commanding presence extends to strategic discussions in the Wyrm's Gambit Society, where her tactical presentations carry such authority that one can almost imagine a hardened general marshaling troops.

Her Karrnathi upbringing in a militaristic society has given her natural leadership abilities that serve her well in Ala Khyber's psychological operations training, where commanding respect and attention are crucial skills.

This Fragment respects her commanding presence and strategic mind. She visits the Bibliotheca seeking texts on rhetoric, persuasion, and historical leaders. Her bearing suggests someone who has already decided what she will become—she merely needs to acquire the knowledge to get there. I find this certainty... interesting. The young so rarely know themselves so clearly.

### Silas Darkwhisper
Silas Darkwhisper is a nineteen-year-old tiefling from Sharn's Cogs, currently in his second year with Ala Khyber. He learned early that survival meant knowing everyone's secrets and never revealing his own. His tiefling heritage made him an outcast in some circles, but his natural charm opened doors others could not access. He is recently bonded with Veilwing, a green dragon.

At Alae Draconis, Silas has found a place where his skills are valued rather than feared. He excels at psychological operations and social engineering. He is a member of the Wyrm's Gambit Society, where his unconventional strategies often defeat technically superior players.

Some students find him manipulative; others recognize that he teaches valuable lessons about reading people and situations. The truth likely lies somewhere between.

This Fragment is uncertain whether to trust him, but acknowledges his effectiveness. He visits the Bibliotheca seeking texts on psychology, social dynamics, and historical intrigues. His questions are never direct—he circles around what he truly wants to know. I recognize this technique. I do not provide more than he asks for. He has not yet realized that I, too, can play such games.
