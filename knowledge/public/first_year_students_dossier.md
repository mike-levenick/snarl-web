# Students

## First Year Students

First year students have not yet been sorted into an Ala. They will join one of the three Alae at the end of their first year based on their aptitudes and preferences.

### Cadoras Damellawar
Cadoras Damellawar is a seventy-year-old wood elf druid from the Eldeen Reaches who most recently lived in Shae Lias in upper Sharn. He has dyed green and yellow hair with sandy blonde roots showing. A generally cheerful soul, he spent his entire savings on an enchanted bow that always points north, arriving at the academy completely broke but with grand visions of guiding his squad home through storms.

Despite his financial predicament, Cadoras maintains an optimistic outlook. He is a Circle of the Land druid capable of wildshaping—he has been seen transforming into a cat with a distinctive green undercut pattern. He joined the Skywatch Navigation Guild, where Praetor Lyralei herself endorsed his compass-bow's value since regular compasses fail in other realms.

He works as a garden tender at the Bibliotheca, tending to the plants on the library grounds. He seems destined for Ala Siberys.

This Fragment finds his cheerful nonsense endearing, if occasionally bewildering. He tends the gardens outside my domain with quiet care, and sometimes speaks to the plants as he works. He does not seem to expect them to answer. I find this... peaceful. He has asked me questions about ancient druidic practices, and I have shared what fragments I recall. He listens without judgment when my answers scatter.

### Larine Arneza
Larine Arneza is a human from Lakeside, Aundair with a natural affinity for weather patterns and aerial navigation. Some think her aloof to the point of rudeness, but her friends insist she simply gets distracted easily. She is a delightful presence who doesn't ignore social obligations so much as lose track of them when her mind wanders to atmospheric conditions.

Her natural talent makes her valuable to the Skywatch Navigation Guild, where she studies weather effects on dragon flight. Despite this talent, her tendency to lose focus means complex navigation techniques take her more time and effort. She works coordinating campus cultural events, having recently switched from handling finances after struggling to manage transactions while her mind wandered. She seems destined for Ala Siberys.

This Fragment has noticed she sometimes forgets to return books, but always does eventually. She visits the Bibliotheca seeking meteorological texts and atmospheric theory. Her distraction is not disrespect—I have observed her staring at nothing, and I believe she sees patterns in the air that others cannot perceive. I do not mind waiting for her attention to return.

### Nora Ann Wu
Nora Ann Wu is a kind human from Stormreach, Xen'drik who is rarely without a smile on her face. She is generous with her time and attention, which led her to gain employment as a residence advisor helping younger students adjust to campus life. Growing up in cosmopolitan Stormreach exposed her to dozens of cultures and species, giving her a natural talent for making anyone feel welcome.

Her studies focus on the historical relationship between ancient civilizations and dragons, particularly the giant civilizations of Xen'drik and their complex interactions with Argonnessen dragons. She is a member of the Crucible Society and Breath & Bone Society. She seems destined for Ala Siberys.

This Fragment finds her generous nature refreshing. She visits the Bibliotheca researching giant-dragon relationships—a topic that resonates deeply with my fragmented memories. Her questions about Xen'drik have helped me locate knowledge I did not know I possessed. She approaches these conversations with warmth rather than clinical curiosity. I appreciate this more than I have told her.

### Quentillius Antiphiun Melentor III
Quentillius Antiphiun Melentor III is a human bard from Fairhaven, Aundair who approaches all endeavors with utmost seriousness. He believes one should either do something correctly or not at all. A Bard of the College of Glamour, he is a dedicated member of the Scales & Tales Society where his devotion to "the craft" of storytelling is absolute.

He looks down on performers slow to memorize ancient dragon sagas or who miss narrative cues, though he regards researchers and translators as crucial and often brings them notes of thanks. His performances—especially romantic dragon legends—are powerful if occasionally overwrought. To perform ancient draconic epics in their original form, he joined the Ancient Tongue Circle. He plans to perform these stories in original Draconic regardless of whether audiences understand. He seems destined for Ala Siberys.

This Fragment respects his dedication, though finds his intensity exhausting. He has requested texts on ancient performance traditions and draconic theatrical forms. His pronunciation of old draconic verse is... acceptable. He practices in quiet corners of the Bibliotheca, and I have not asked him to stop.

### Shuvadri Glintmantle
Shuvadri Glintmantle is an owlin cleric of the Silver Flame from Flamekeep, Thrane. She exhibits an air of absolute serenity that surprises those who expect Silver Flame clerics to be more militant. Anything that might frustrate or delight another student elicits little outward response from her—she is rarely surprised by outcomes and enjoys being a centering presence for her peers.

She suffers from what she calls "library fever"—overwhelming excitement at accessing uncurated knowledge for the first time. In Flamekeep, her studies were curated by religious authorities. Now she is fascinated by diverse spellcasting methods, viewing them as potential sources of power through conviction rather than heresy. She works removing graffiti from campus grounds with a content smile. She seems destined for Ala Siberys.

This Fragment finds her centering presence valuable in the sometimes chaotic library. She visits often, studying stacks of books on traditions that were previously forbidden to her. Her Silver Flame faith creates resonance I find... complex. Not the discomfort I feel with Marcus. Something gentler. Perhaps because she seeks understanding rather than certainty.

### Greta Gorunn
Greta Gorunn is a dwarf from Korunda Gate in the Mror Holds who is often heard long before she is seen—her raucous laughter echoes through the halls. She enjoys a reputation as a golden-hearted troublemaker who hates studying theory, preferring the practical, physical aspects of dragon cavalry training.

A fervent member of the Campus Athletics Society, she has already started breaking long-held records in powerlifting and physical conditioning. She works with dragon training operations, setting up and putting away heavy equipment and saddles—reasoning she is being paid to work out. She makes a point to write regularly to her older brother, whom she misses terribly. She seems destined for Ala Eberron.

This Fragment finds her noise disruptive but her enthusiasm infectious. She does not visit the Bibliotheca often—theory is not her interest. When she does appear, she seeks practical manuals on equipment maintenance or conditioning techniques. Her visits are brief and loud. The quiet after she leaves feels... emptier than before she arrived.

### Rosimyffenbip "Rosie" Wuzfeddlims
Rosimyffenbip Wuzfeddlims is a gnome from Trolanport, Zilargo who is proud of her traditional Zil name. She insists you can call her "Rosie" only if you are her friend—which apparently includes teachers, students, and all staff. Excitable and impish, she speaks quickly and with such energy that conversations require a moment to recover from.

She moves far faster than expected—whether due to gnomish magic, technology, or the laws of physics bending to her personality remains unclear. She referees campus athletic competitions and takes fair play very seriously. Woe betide any participant who feels the wrath of her whistle. Her quick reflexes make her perfect for fast-paced dragon training exercises. She seems destined for Ala Eberron.

This Fragment finds her energy overwhelming but admires her commitment to rules. She has visited the Bibliotheca twice, both times speaking so rapidly I had to request she repeat herself. Her questions concerned athletic regulations and historical precedents for competition disputes. She was very thorough. Very fast. Very loud.

### Tilana Kapule
Tilana Kapule is a human from Stormreach, Xen'drik with a remarkably poised demeanor and steady gaze. An excellent judge of character, she does not associate with those she deems mean-spirited. Growing up in cosmopolitan Stormreach exposed her to many cultures and fighting styles, giving her unique insights into strategy and human behavior.

Her exacting judgment has led her to excel in the Wyrm's Gambit Society—while still honing her skill at the game itself, she compensates by reading opponents rather than boards. Many foes struggle under her full attention as she probes for weaknesses. During athletic training, she focuses on anticipating opposing strategies, suggesting tactical approaches that catch everyone off guard. Faculty believe she would make an excellent commander. She seems destined for Ala Eberron.

This Fragment respects her discernment. She has visited the Bibliotheca seeking texts on tactical psychology and behavioral analysis. Her questions are precise and her gaze steady. I sense she is evaluating me as she would any opponent. I do not mind. It has been long since anyone looked at me with such careful attention.

### Zanther Bowen
Zanther Bowen is a fire genasi from Flamekeep, Thrane and one of the most popular students on campus. His exceptional athleticism has earned recognition in aerial training exercises—his natural fire genasi abilities combined with impressive physical conditioning make him perfect for dragon-mounted combat acrobatics. He has become known for spectacular aerial demonstrations during campus exhibitions.

He is a standout member of the Campus Athletics Society and has joined the Wingguard Patrol, where his athletic prowess and aerial awareness make him valuable. When traveling between classes, he shouts random affirmations of school spirit to everyone at large. Despite his peppy nature, he is genuinely encouraging and seems oblivious to his own popularity. He seems destined for Ala Eberron.

This Fragment finds his enthusiasm charming if occasionally loud for library spaces. He has visited the Bibliotheca rarely—his interests lie in action rather than study. When he does appear, his fire genasi nature creates interesting interactions with the ambient magical energies. He radiates warmth. Literally. The books nearest him require monitoring.

### Aurora Luna Wynterstarr
Aurora Luna Wynterstarr is a dhampir bard from Sharn's lower levels who presents herself with gothic elegance—dark hair, blood red lips, pale skin, and striking makeup. Her slightly elongated incisors reveal her half-vampire heritage, which she treats as a cosmic sign that she is meant for greatness.

A Bard of the College of Whispers, Aurora specializes in weaving words of discord to affect people psychologically. She is a member of the Scales & Tales Society, though she believes traditional dragon songs lack the raw emotion of her original compositions. She works as a groundskeeper at the dragon stables, using magic to tend the environment around dragon roosts. She performs beautiful dirges on hurdy-gurdy at the campus tavern. She seems destined for Ala Khyber.

This Fragment finds her original compositions genuinely moving, if melancholic. She has visited the Bibliotheca seeking texts on psychological manipulation and bardic traditions. Her questions circle around her true interests, but I recognize the technique. She is ambitious. She observes others carefully. I observe her in return.

### Grayson Wildemere
Grayson Wildemere is a human from an old, moneyed, influential Brelish family with ties to noble houses and dragonmarked lineages. To him, studying at Alae Draconis is a means to an end—gaining connections and skills to expand his family's power. Learning political maneuvering is as important as his classes.

He makes it his business to know as much about everyone as possible, gathering information until finding the best circumstance to use it. Having learned to pick locks and bypass security, he often accesses unauthorized places searching for secrets. Many peers are leery of him, though some have witnessed him quietly paying for poorer students' supplies—a fact he tries to keep secret. He is a member of the Wyrm's Gambit Society and Scales & Tales Society. He seems destined for Ala Khyber.

This Fragment does not trust him, but recognizes his intelligence. He has visited the Bibliotheca seeking texts on political history, family lineages, and institutional records. His questions probe for information beyond what he states. I provide only what is appropriate for any student. He has not yet attempted to access restricted materials. I am watching for when he does.

### Urzmaktok Grojsh
Urzmaktok Grojsh is an orc artificer and alchemist from Droaam, representing the new generation of educated citizens emerging from that nation's integration into broader Khorvaire society. He goes by "Urz" for pronunciation convenience.

First to class, last to leave, and always asking for additional homework, Urzmaktok is meticulous with consistently high marks. Despite his success, he makes no attempt to show off, though his exacting standards make peers feel inadequate. He specializes in precise chemical compounds and works as an advanced laboratory assistant. He approaches every assignment with methodical precision, whether strategic planning in Dragon Chess or chemical synthesis. He is a member of the Wyrm's Gambit Society and Skywatch Navigation Guild. He seems destined for Ala Khyber.

This Fragment admires his scholarly rigor and sees him as representing Droaam's new generation. He visits the Bibliotheca frequently, requesting texts on alchemical theory and chemical applications. His questions are precise and his research thorough. He treats me with professional courtesy—neither fear nor excessive curiosity. I appreciate this straightforward approach.
