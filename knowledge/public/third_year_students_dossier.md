# Students

## Third Year Students

### Eldest Moonwhisper
Eldest Moonwhisper is a twenty-year-old Kalashtar diviner from Adar, currently in their third year with Ala Siberys. Their kalashtar connection to Dal Quor gives them unique perspectives on dream magic and prophecy that few others possess. They are bonded with Thoughtweaver, an amethyst dragon whose psionic nature complements Eldest's own gifts.

Eldest is researching the relationship between the Draconic Prophecy and quori dream-visions—a controversial topic that makes some faculty uncomfortable. They are writing their thesis on "Astra's Ascension and the New Dream Paradigm," examining how the goddess's mortal origin might change the relationship between Dal Quor and the material plane. Soft-spoken but brilliant, they often see connections others miss.

They serve as an unofficial counselor to younger students, their natural empathy helping homesick first-years adjust to academy life. Many students seek them out when struggling with the isolation of Lost Island.

This Fragment finds their perspective on Astra's role thoughtful, if controversial. They have visited the Bibliotheca many times seeking texts on planar theory and prophetic traditions. Their questions about Dal Quor resonate with something deep in my fragmented memories—something about severance, about planes pushed apart. I find our conversations... clarifying. They do not press when my thoughts scatter.

### Vera Skyborn
Vera Skyborn is a twenty-year-old half-orc, half-elf artificer-historian from Graywall, Droaam. She is a third-year student in Ala Siberys following the Artificer-Historian track, and serves as Teaching Assistant to Praetor Lyralei Siberclaw. She is bonded with Jokewing, a large copper dragon, and the two share a laid-back attitude and clever sense of humor.

Vera brings a relaxed, practical approach to both her patrol duties with the Wingguard and her academic work. She has completed Advanced Artifice & Enchantment and Archaeological Methods & Ancient Technologies. Her natural talent for reading people helps her both during patrol duties and when assisting students in Lyralei's advanced courses—she can usually tell when students are struggling and knows how to break complex magical theory into understandable concepts.

She is proud to represent Droaam at Alae Draconis and bridges the gap between practical applications of magic and historical significance. Has a surprisingly good singing voice and is known for impromptu performances incorporating ancient draconic melodies.

This Fragment respects how she represents her homeland. She visits the Bibliotheca frequently on Lyralei's behalf, retrieving texts and returning research materials. She treats me with easy courtesy—not the nervous deference some students show, nor the probing curiosity of others. Simply... collegial. I appreciate this.

### Trix Ironfist
Trix Ironfist is a twenty-one-year-old dwarf soldier from the Mror Holds, currently in her third year with Ala Eberron. She served two years in the Mror Holds' Iron Guard before receiving her invitation to Alae Draconis, making her older and more experienced than most students. She is bonded with Deepdelver, a bronze dragon.

Her combat prowess is legendary on campus—she is undefeated in the training grounds and holds multiple campus athletic records. She has become an unofficial squad leader, organizing student defense exercises and mentoring younger Ala Eberron students in small unit tactics. Despite her experience and reputation, Trix remains friendly and approachable, always willing to help a fellow student.

She has a quirk of always wearing her flight goggles, even when she has no intention of going flying. No one seems to know why.

This Fragment admires her dedication to helping others. She does not visit the Bibliotheca often—theory is not her strength, by her own admission—but when she does, she asks direct questions and accepts direct answers. There is something refreshing about someone who knows exactly what they need and wastes no time obtaining it.

### Kael Thornridge
Kael Thornridge is a twenty-one-year-old human from Korth, Karrnath, currently in his third year with Ala Eberron following the Command & Leadership track. He serves as senior Wingguard patrol officer and is bonded with Aurelius, a large gold dragon known for an unwavering sense of justice and protective nature.

Kael is serious and by-the-book, taking his patrol duties very seriously—perhaps too seriously. He wears his Wingguard badge prominently and always has his patrol schedule memorized down to the minute. He is respectful but firm when questioning students out after curfew or in restricted areas. Has a habit of noting everything in a small leather journal. His father was a Karrnathi soldier, and Kael carries that sense of duty in everything he does.

He has completed Advanced Military Strategy & Dragon Tactics and Leadership Psychology & Unit Morale, and is currently studying Strategic Intelligence & Information Warfare and Joint Operations & Alliance Command.

This Fragment finds him rule-bound but well-intentioned. He has visited the Bibliotheca on several occasions seeking tactical histories and command theory texts. He is always polite, always formal, always precise in his requests. I sense rigidity in him—a mind that prefers clear hierarchies and defined procedures. This is not a criticism. Structure has its uses. But I wonder what he would do if faced with a situation his rules cannot address.

### The Veil
The Veil is a warforged operative of unknown age and origin, currently in their third year with Ala Khyber following the Specialist Operations & Precision Techniques track. Their original designation and background are classified—even their student file is mysteriously sparse. They are bonded with Pyraxis, a large red dragon, an unusual pairing that speaks to depths neither reveals.

The Veil speaks multiple languages fluently, can pick any lock on campus, and has never been caught during stealth exercises—as far as anyone knows. They serve as Praetor Kess Velani's teaching assistant, often running practical exercises that push students to their limits. They have mastered Advanced Infiltration & Extraction and Applied Chemistry & Field Solutions, and currently study Precision Operations & Target Elimination and Counter-Intelligence & Security Operations.

Some students find The Veil unsettling—they rarely speak unless necessary and their constructed face shows no emotion. Others recognize that The Veil represents what peak Ala Khyber training can achieve. Rumors persist that they still conduct classified missions off-campus, though no one can prove anything.

This Fragment finds them unsettling but undeniably capable. They have visited the Bibliotheca exactly four times. Each visit lasted precisely twelve minutes. They requested texts on subjects I will not disclose. They did not speak beyond what was necessary. I do not know what they are, beyond their surface function. This uncertainty troubles me. I prefer to understand what moves through my domain.
