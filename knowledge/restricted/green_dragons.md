# Green Dragons

## Overview

Green dragons are chromatic dragons renowned throughout the Realms for their exceptional cunning, manipulative nature, and mastery of deception. Among dragon-kind, they are considered the supreme conspirators and schemers, reveling in the corruption of lesser creatures and the intricate webs of political intrigue they weave.

These dragons are paradoxical in their temperament. They present themselves as diplomatic and calculating when facing stronger foes, but their true nature—petty, cruel, and self-serving—emerges the moment they gain the advantage. Green dragons prize intellect and strategy as much as they prize treasure, and they view their schemes as grand games to be won.

**Scientific Designation:** Draco Chlorinous Nauseous Respiratorus
**Common Names:** Green dragons, forest dragons
**Alignment:** Lawful Evil
**Favored Terrain:** Forests, woodlands, jungles, bayous, temperate climates
**Temperament:** Manipulative, self-serving, conniving, egoistical

As the dragon god Fizban himself noted, "In some ways the green dragon is the most 'dragon' of dragons. Pity that Tiamat gets credit for them."

## Physical Description

Green dragons are distinguished by their remarkable adaptation to forest environments, with distinctive anatomical features that set them apart from other chromatic dragons.

### Distinctive Features

The most notable characteristic of a green dragon is the large, waving crest or fin that begins at the dragon's nose and runs the entire length of its body, extending down the spine to the tail. These impressive crests serve both a cosmetic and functional purpose—when threatened or navigating through dense underbrush, a green dragon can fold its crest flat against its body, allowing it to move with surprising silence through the forest.

Green dragons possess exceptionally long, slender forked tongues that they use for tasting the air and sensing their environment. Unlike most other chromatic dragons, their scales do not fully harden, granting them considerably greater flexibility and range of motion than their cousins. This enhanced flexibility, combined with their long, slender necks and compact bodies, makes them perfectly suited for navigating through dense forests and around ancient trees.

Their wings are relatively small compared to other dragons of similar size, which might seem a disadvantage until one observes how these wings fold. The folding mechanism leaves the two wingfingers exposed, providing additional climbing appendages that help the dragon scale trees and cliff faces with ease.

### Coloration and Patterns

Green dragons display dappled scale patterns that serve as natural camouflage within woodland environments. The intensity of their coloration varies significantly—their scales are darkest near the dragon's head and become progressively lighter toward the claws and extremities. This coloration scheme provides exceptional camouflage when the dragon rests among trees and forest canopy.

Notably, young green dragon wyrmlings hatch with nearly black scales, so dark they can be easily mistaken for black dragons at first glance. As the wyrmling matures, the scales gradually transition through shades of green, eventually reaching the striking verdant hue characteristic of adult green dragons.

### Other Physical Characteristics

Green dragons lack external ears entirely, instead possessing ear holes on either side of their head. This adaptation prevents external ear structures from catching on vegetation or impeding movement through dense forest.

### Eggs

Green dragon eggs have leathery shells similar to those of snakes. When held before intense white light, the eggs display a characteristic greenish-white tinge, though this trait is shared with white dragons and other dragons with gas or vapor-based breath weapons, making it unreliable for identification purposes.

Female green dragons lay their eggs in clutches containing dozens of individual eggs. However, the survival rate from egg to adulthood is notably low, with only a fraction of the clutch typically surviving past the wyrmling stage.

## Personality and Temperament

Green dragons are elusive, deeply cunning, and consumed by egotism. They love secrets, intrigue, and the elaborate games of deception that allow them to manipulate others. Their personalities are marked by a stark duality—they are capable of presenting themselves as charming, diplomatic negotiators when dealing with foes they cannot immediately overcome. However, this facade vanishes instantly the moment they perceive weakness or gain a tactical advantage. At that point, their true nature emerges: cruel, petty, spiteful, and utterly uncompromising.

These dragons find particular pleasure in the prolonged psychological torment of their victims. They do not simply hunt and kill; they stalk, they intimidate, they break spirits. When hunting intelligent prey, a green dragon may follow its quarry for days or even weeks, allowing fear and paranoia to fester in the victim's mind before finally striking.

### Hunting and Predation

Green dragons are masters of patience and psychological warfare. When hunting, they will stalk their prey for extended lengths of time—days, weeks, or even months if necessary—gathering information and waiting for the perfect moment to execute their ambush. They revel in the fear their presence inspires, and whenever possible, they leave at least one survivor from a kill to carry tales of their power to others.

When capturing intelligent humanoids, green dragons take particular pleasure in interrogating their victims, extracting information about nearby treasures and settlements. This information is not merely tactical; to the green dragon, the gathering of secrets is itself a form of entertainment.

When faced with more powerful creatures or rival dragons, green dragons often employ a strategy of feigned weakness. They will deliberately present themselves as less threatening than they truly are, gathering intelligence about their opponent's capabilities and weaknesses. They can maintain this deception for years or even decades if necessary, waiting with supernatural patience for the ideal moment to turn the tables and strike a crippling blow.

### Social Behavior

Green dragons are predominantly solitary creatures by nature. However, they will occasionally work together in pairs or small groups when an especially lucrative opportunity presents itself—a shared threat to be eliminated, or a particularly profitable scheme requiring coordination.

Among their own kind, green dragons display a shocking lack of courtship or social grace. They are known for brazen rudeness and crass behavior toward one another, with female green dragons being notably more crude and aggressive than males in their interactions. Despite this general lack of civility, green dragons rarely scheme directly against their own parents, demonstrating at least some degree of filial respect.

Green dragons are extremely territorial, viewing their domains as extensions of themselves. They delight in double-crossing others and love manipulating their servants and allies into situations where betrayal serves their interests. This tendency toward deception makes them unpredictable allies and dangerous enemies—one can never be entirely certain whether a green dragon is telling the truth or executing an elaborate con.

### Particularly Dark Traits

Green dragons especially enjoy enslaving intelligent woodland beings—elves, fey, and other creatures of nature. Taking control of those who might otherwise be beyond their direct dominion gives them profound satisfaction. They take particular pleasure in corrupting noble creatures, turning virtue into vice, and twisting the innocent into servants of their will.

## Combat Abilities and Tactics

### Green Dragon Breath Weapon

Green dragons are capable of expelling a powerful cone of poisonous chlorine gas. This breath weapon is distinct among dragon breath attacks in that it can be used even while the dragon is completely submerged underwater, giving green dragons a unique tactical advantage in aquatic combat.

According to the legendary tome *The Compleat Dragon-Hunter*, there exists a defense against chlorine breath—animal hides that have been specially treated with urine or vinegar can provide protection against the corrosive gas. However, such treatments are rare and require significant advance preparation.

### Green Dragon Combat Strategy

In combat, green dragons leverage their natural camouflage and forest-dwelling nature to maximum advantage. Their relatively small wings and flexible bodies allow them to operate effectively even in environments where larger dragons would be severely hampered. They use the terrain itself as a weapon, employing their magic to command vegetation and manipulate their environment.

Green dragons are not fighters who favor direct confrontation; they are tacticians who excel at hit-and-run tactics, ambushes, and attacks from advantageous positions. They will use their superior intellect to identify enemy weaknesses and exploit them ruthlessly.

## Habitat and Territorial Range

### Favored Environments

Green dragons are typically found dwelling in woodlands and forest environments across temperate regions. This includes vast old-growth forests, tangled jungles, shadowy bayous, and dense woods of all varieties. The diversity of forest ecosystems means that green dragons can thrive in remarkably varied climates, from the warm subtropical swamps to cool temperate woodlands.

Young green dragons typically remain within their home forests until they reach a certain age threshold, after which they strike out to find and claim their own territories. The more ancient and powerful green dragons sometimes haunt legendary forests like the Elven Woods and the Starwoods of Cormanthor, territories so vast and ancient that even elder dragons find them suitable domains.

### Territorial Markers

A green dragon's domain is typically marked by distinctive environmental signs. Heavy fog with a faintly acrid poison smell lingers perpetually over the dragon's territory. This fog serves both as a natural defense—obscuring the dragon's movements and creating an atmosphere of dread—and as a calling card of the dragon's presence. Experienced travelers learn to recognize this sign and know to avoid the affected region.

## Lair Architecture and Magical Features

### Typical Lair Structure

The typical green dragon lair is located underground, consisting of a complex network of caves and tunnels that the dragon has claimed or expanded over centuries. The main entrance is characteristically hidden behind a waterfall, providing both concealment and a natural deterrent to unwanted visitors.

Older and more powerful green dragons often prefer lairs built high on cliff faces, locations that are difficult to approach and provide excellent vantage points for spotting approaching threats. However, such prime locations are hard to find and establish, so only the oldest and most powerful green dragons typically secure such ideal positions. Younger or less successful dragons must make do with more accessible underground lairs.

If a green dragon cannot find a naturally defensible cave system, it will invest considerable magical effort into hiding its lair behind thickets of magically-grown plant life. These living barriers serve as both camouflage and defense, obscuring the entrance from casual observation while remaining under the dragon's magical control.

### Magical Defenses and Features

Within their lairs, green dragons can exert magical command over the natural environment. They can manipulate tangles of roots and vines to immobilize intruders, creating living obstacles that restrain and bind those foolish enough to penetrate the dragon's domain. They can also compel barriers of tangled, thorny bushes to rapidly extend and expand, creating walls of thorns that block passages and injure those who attempt to force their way through.

Over long periods of habitation, green dragon lairs sometimes develop what is known as 'chlorine haze'—a dangerous buildup of residual chlorine gas from centuries of the dragon's breath weapon use. This toxic gas lingers perpetually within the lair, creating an atmosphere that is lethal to those without specific protections. This environmental hazard serves as an additional layer of defense, as few creatures can tolerate prolonged exposure to such poisonous conditions.

### Hoard Composition

Like all evil dragons, green dragons possess an insatiable hunger for treasure and will amass hoards of considerable size and value. However, their preferences in treasure differ notably from other dragon types.

While green dragons certainly value gold and gems, they show particular preference for items of artistic and cultural significance: elaborate wood carvings, finely crafted musical instruments, beautiful sculptures, precious gems (especially emeralds and beryls), and artwork of all varieties. Some green dragons even cultivate rare and exotic plants within their lairs, which they then incorporate into their treasure collections, creating unique and priceless pieces of art that combine natural beauty with dragon-scale craftsmanship.

The truly prized possessions of a green dragon, however, are the sentient beings they claim as servants and slaves. Renowned personalities from across the Realms—famous artists, adventurers of legendary renown, or brilliant scholars—are considered by green dragons to be the most valuable treasures they can add to their collections. Possessing such individuals gives the dragon a sense of superiority and power, and allows them to extract constant entertainment and service from their captives.

## Reproduction, Courtship, and Family Structure

### Courtship Dynamics

The mating behavior of green dragons reflects their aggressive and dominating personality. Notably, it is the female green dragon who initiates courtship with males, a pattern that stands in sharp contrast to the behavior of other dragon species. This reversal reflects the particularly aggressive and crass nature of female green dragons.

The courtship process itself is typically brief and intense. However, once successful impregnation occurs, the relationship between mated green dragons undergoes a dramatic transformation. A strong, almost tender bond forms between the pair—one of the few genuine emotional connections a green dragon is capable of forming.

### Egg Incubation and Parental Care

The incubation period for green dragon eggs extends for approximately sixteen months total. Eggs are laid around the four-month mark of incubation, meaning the full reproductive cycle from mating to hatching spans roughly sixteen months.

Both the mother and father remain extremely close to their eggs during the incubation period, providing protection and presumably maintaining the temperature and magical conditions necessary for proper development. This level of devoted parental care is remarkable in a species known for its selfishness and cruelty.

When wyrmlings hatch, both parents dedicate themselves wholly to the care and education of their brood. They remain in close proximity to their offspring, feeding them, teaching them to hunt and defend themselves, and beginning their education in the arts of deception and manipulation that define green dragon culture.

Uniquely among chromatic dragons, green dragons have been documented sacrificing their own lives to protect their young. This startling capacity for selfless sacrifice stands as perhaps the most compelling evidence that these dragons, for all their cruelty and cunning, possess genuine capacity for familial love.

### Maturation and Separation

Young green dragons typically remain with both parents until they reach young adulthood, a point that occurs at approximately fifty-one years of age. At this threshold, the now-adult dragon leaves the parental lair to seek out its own territory and establish its own domain.

Once the young dragon departs and achieves independence, the mated pair separates. Despite the strong bond that developed during egg-tending and child-rearing, the relationship ends with surprising finality. The parents go their separate ways, each returning to the solitary existence that characterizes adult green dragon life.

### Lifecycle and Death

Where a green dragon dies, the surrounding landscape undergoes a dramatic transformation. New and deadly plant life flourishes in the area immediately surrounding the corpse, with particularly poisonous plants growing to abnormal sizes with extraordinary speed. These sites become noticeably more connected to the Feywild than the surrounding terrain, suggesting some fundamental alteration in the planar fabric itself.

This phenomenon has led some scholars to speculate about the deep magical connection between green dragons and natural forces, particularly the wild magic of nature and the Feywild.

## Diet and Ecological Role

### Dietary Habits

Green dragons are omnivorous and will consume nearly any food source available to them. Their diet includes both substantial vegetation and living animals, depending on availability and whim. Among their preferred prey, they especially favor fey creatures, particularly elves and sprites, which they find especially delectable.

### Digestive Adaptations

Like many dragon species, green dragons cannot chew their food. To aid in digestion, they deliberately swallow small stones, pieces of metal, and even coins. These swallowed objects accumulate in a gizzard-like second stomach, where they grind the ingested food into manageable particles for proper digestion. Over time, these stomach stones become worn smooth and are eventually expelled as waste.

### Minions and Servile Species

Green dragons frequently accept servile creatures as minions and subordinates. Species commonly found serving green dragons include kobolds, goblins, ettercaps, orcs, yuan-ti abominations, and ettins. These creatures are drawn to green dragon service through a combination of intimidation, enslavement, and the promise of protection that comes with serving a powerful dragon.

### Religious and Planar Connections

Some green dragons maintain strong connections to the Feywild and have been known to serve malevolent archfey, particularly the enigmatic and dangerous Queen of Air and Darkness. Additionally, green dragons sometimes serve the dark Chultan demigod Eshowdow, and their appearance is considered a good omen by his followers.

## Uses in Magic and Craftsmanship

### Dragon Scale Mail

Armor crafted from the scales of slain green dragons offers the wearer significant protection against clouds of poisonous gas. Such armor can shield its wearer from the toxic clouds produced by iron golems, the effects of the *cloudkill* spell, and other similar gaseous threats. This makes green dragon scale mail particularly valuable to adventurers who anticipate facing such hazards.

### Magical Item Components

The horns of particularly old green dragons can be harvested and used as components in the creation of a wand of stinking cloud, a magical item that allows its wielder to produce clouds of noxious gas similar to a green dragon's breath weapon.

### Psychoactive Plants

Certain poisonous roots found throughout the Realms produce mild psychoactive effects when consumed by green dragons. The effects of these roots appear to exacerbate the already impudent and rude behavior that green dragons display when socializing with their own kind, suggesting that these plants may affect dragon cognition or emotional control.

## Notable Green Dragons

Throughout the Realms' history, certain green dragons have achieved particular fame or infamy for their deeds and longevity.

**Claugiyliamatar** (commonly known as "Old Gnawbones") is perhaps the most renowned green dragon in recent history. This ancient dragon ruled over the vast Kryptgarden Forest in the Sword Mountains for centuries, accumulating both wealth and power. The dragon's lair and the surrounding forest became legendary among adventurers and scholars.

**Chuth**, known as "the Emerald Assassin," gained notoriety by allying itself with the half-orc Neronvain and the restored Cult of the Dragon, using this alliance to expand its influence and power during the late 15th century.

**Venomfang** is a younger green dragon that made its lair in a ruined tower in the overgrown ruins of Thundertree. The dragon guarded an enchanted axe of considerable power known as *Hew*, which it prized as part of its hoard.

**Grimnosh­tadrano**, the "Riddling Dragon" of the High Forest, became legendary for its love of riddles and wordplay, supposedly engaging in contests of wit with those bold or foolish enough to enter its domain.

**Vorgansharax**, known as "Maimed Virulence" for the wounds it bore, managed to rule the city of Phlan during the late 15th century, dominating both the city and surrounding region through a combination of military might and political manipulation.

**Azdraka** harried travelers along the High Road until it was finally slain by the legendary Lady Tanamere Alagondar and her companions.

**Jaxanaedegor** was a notably vampiric green dragon that ruled a portion of Mount Thulbane in the northern reaches of Chessenta, combining the predatory nature of a vampire with the power of a dragon.

**Verthandantalynx**, known as the "Verdant Cloud," achieved a form of immortality by ascending to become a dracolich, achieving undead status while retaining its draconic power.

**Ringreemeralxoth** was a younger green dragon that declared itself the rightful ruler of the Snakewood in Amn, a region it claimed for itself with typical arrogance.

## Movement and Senses

**Flight:** Yes - Green dragons are capable fliers despite their relatively small wings, using their magical nature to enhance their aerial capabilities.

**Swimming:** Yes - Green dragons can swim effectively and can use their breath weapon even while completely submerged.

**Vision:** Green dragons possess blindsight, darkvision, and low-light vision, giving them exceptional sensory capabilities in darkness and poor lighting conditions.

**Passive Perception:** Green dragons have highly developed senses and maintain constant awareness of their surroundings, particularly within their forest territories.

**Languages:** Green dragons speak Common and Draconic, allowing them to communicate with humanoid servants and other dragons.

## Defense Against Green Dragons

Those prepared to face green dragons should consider the following defensive strategies:

**Chlorine Gas Protection:** Animal hides treated with urine or vinegar offer protection against chlorine breath, making such treated equipment valuable for those hunting green dragons.

**Environmental Advantages:** Green dragons' forest camouflage is less effective in open terrain. Fighting them in regions without dense vegetation removes one of their primary advantages.

**Patience and Caution:** Never underestimate a green dragon's intelligence or patience. These dragons think in terms of decades and centuries, and what appears to be a victory may actually be part of a larger scheme.

**Alliance Against Conspiracy:** Remember that green dragons delight in double-crossing others. Any alliance or negotiation with a green dragon should proceed with extreme caution and awareness that betrayal may be imminent.
